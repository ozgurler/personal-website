Get Hired with Claude: A Job Seeker's Field Guide

Claude can search Indeed's live job listings, dig into companies, and tailor your resume — all in one conversation. Here's how to set it up in five minutes, plus the settings that make it stick.

Part 1 — Setup

Connect Indeed in 5 minutes

The Indeed connector is an official integration that lets Claude search Indeed's job database in real time — no tab-switching, no copy-pasting listings.

  1. Open ClaudeGo to claude.ai or open the Claude desktop app.
  2. Find the connectors menuIn the chat box, click the Search & Tools menu, then choose + Add connectors.
  3. Add IndeedSelect Indeed from the list of available connectors.
  4. Sign inLog in with your Indeed account (or create one). Done — Claude can now search Indeed for you.

Part 2 — What it can do

Four superpowers, one chat

Once connected, Claude can pull four kinds of information straight from Indeed:

Job Search

Find openings by title, keywords, location, and employment type — with companies, salaries, and direct application links.

Job Details

Pull the full posting: description, requirements, qualifications, and benefits for any listing.

Your Resume

Read the experience on your Indeed profile so results and advice are personalized to you.

Company Research

Check employer reviews — satisfaction, pay, culture, and management — before you apply or interview.

Part 3 — Prompts that work

Say this, get results

Upload your resume (or let Claude read your Indeed profile), then try these. Swap the [brackets] for your details.

Here's my resume. Search Indeed for [job title] roles in [city / remote] posted in the last week. Rank the top 10 by how well they match my experience, and tell me why.

The ranked shortlist — your daily starting point.

Pull the full details for job [#3]. List the requirements I clearly meet, the gaps I have, and how I'd address each gap in a cover letter.

The gap analysis — know your pitch before you write a word.

Research [company name] on Indeed — reviews, compensation, culture, management. Give me the honest picture and three smart questions to ask in an interview.

The background check — walk into interviews informed.

Tailor my resume summary and bullet points to this job description. Keep every claim truthful — just reframe my real experience using the language the posting uses.

The tailored resume — matched to the posting, still 100% you.

Part 4 — Handy configurations

Settings that make it stick

Create a Job Hunt Project

Put your resume, target role list, and cover-letter template into one Project. Every chat inside it starts with your full context already loaded.

Turn on memory

With memory enabled in settings, Claude remembers your search across conversations — no re-explaining your background every morning.

Connect Google Drive

Add the Google Drive connector so Claude can pull your latest resume or portfolio straight from your files.

Set your writing style

Use styles and preferences to lock in the tone you want — concise and confident cover letters, every time.

Schedule a daily digest Paid plans

On Pro and above, scheduled tasks can run a morning job search automatically. Results can vary with connectors, so spot-check the first few runs.

Part 5 — Ground rules

Play it smart

  • Quality beats volume. Ten tailored applications outperform a hundred generic ones. Use Claude to go deeper, not just wider.
  • You hit "apply," not the AI. Claude surfaces listings with direct links — reviewing and submitting each application yourself keeps quality (and honesty) high.
  • Keep everything truthful. Tailoring means reframing real experience, never inventing it. Say so in your prompts.
  • Verify before you commit. Double-check salaries, dates, and details on the live Indeed listing — postings change fast.
  • Prep like a pro. After the search, ask Claude to run a mock interview based on the exact job description.
