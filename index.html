About

From silicon to strategy

Most salespeople learn the pitch. I learned the product first. I trained as an electronics and communication engineer, spent years in presales answering million-dollar RFPs line by line, and am currently completing a microelectronics program — because I believe the best advisors understand technology from fabrication level up.

That foundation is why my clients trust the recommendation, not just the demo. Across telco infrastructure, board-management SaaS, AI-driven ad bidding, and enterprise content management, my job has been the same: bridge what a business needs with the technology that actually delivers it — without costly, misaligned investments.

Today I lead business development at FLYONIT, helping Pacific Northwest businesses with managed IT, cybersecurity, and cloud services. If your technology is holding you back instead of driving you forward, let's talk.
